# Assessing the intro video

Reached from Step 2, **only if** a video is to be assessed. Without a video the audit carries on
as normal, and the missing video becomes a finding like any other, with a consequence and a fix.

## Why a detour is needed

Neither the MCP nor the profile page hands over the video file. The path runs through the user:
they download their own intro video and give the file path. That is a request, not a blocker,
and **asking once is enough.** Anyone who declines the first time does not get pushed again on
every later run; instead the missing verdict gets noted as an open item.

## The process

1. Ask for the file, with the reason in half a sentence: without the file you can only assess
   *whether* a video exists, not *whether it works*.
2. Run the **`video-analyzer`** skill on the file. It breaks the video into frames, detects scene
   changes and transcribes the audio into a markdown timeline. The assessment comes from
   transcript plus frames, so the video never has to be played.
3. Judge against the criteria below and file the finding into the same list as all the others.

## What an intro video is measured on

| What | What it hangs on |
|---|---|
| **The first seconds** | Do they say what the viewer gets out of this, or is it a greeting? Same logic as the first two lines of the overview: the opening decides whether the rest runs. |
| **Positioning** | Does the niche land early and in plain words? A video that leaves you unable to say what this person does after 20 seconds works against the profile. |
| **Agreement with the profile** | Does the video say the same thing as the title and overview? A contradiction costs more here than anywhere else, because face and voice carry more weight than text. |
| **Speech and pace** | Understandable, without a carpet of filler words, without a read-aloud tone. Readable straight from the transcript. |
| **Length** | Does the content carry the runtime? A tight short video beats a complete long one. |
| **Picture and sound** | From the frames: lighting, framing, background. Sound: noise, echo. Production is the cheapest part and the most obvious one when it is missing. |
| **The ending** | Does it end on a clear next step, or does it just trail off? |

## The fix

As with every finding, a concrete suggestion belongs with it. For a video that usually is not a
reshoot but a new opening or a cut. If a reshoot is warranted, a script comes with it, written in
spoken language rather than prose, and in whatever language the profile is in.
