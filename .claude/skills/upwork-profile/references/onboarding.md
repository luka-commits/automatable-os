# No profile yet: guiding the setup

Reached from Step 1, when `get_profile action=get` returns nothing or an empty profile.

This is not a different skill and not a different standard. It is the same audit without any
existing material. The table in Step 2 of SKILL.md still says what makes a field good; what
follows here is only the order in which the fields come into being, and what runs differently.

## The order is not arbitrary

Positioning first, then the fields. Whoever starts with the title field writes a title for a
niche that has not been decided yet, and then writes it again.

1. **Settle the niche.** What kind of project, for what kind of client. Two or three questions
   are enough, and the answer is one line, not a concept. Without it, everything after is
   guesswork.
2. **Collect the evidence.** What has been built already, for whom, with what outcome? Including
   work outside Upwork: the first presence lives on work that happened before it. The result goes
   into `context/experience.md` and from then on is the source for the
   overview, the portfolio and later every proposal.
3. **Title.** Five to eight variants with different angles, to pick from.
4. **Overview.** Write the first two lines first and show them on their own: they carry the
   decision, the rest is evidence.
5. **Skills** derived from the niche, not from everything else you happen to be able to do.
6. **Portfolio:** two projects with an outcome in the title beat six without.
7. **Rate:** as a starting range with a reason, not a number pulled from thin air.
8. **The rest:** education, employment, languages, availability. These four run through
   `update_profile`, each after an explicit yes.

## What runs differently here than in an audit

**Questions come bundled, not one at a time.** Eight steps with one follow-up question each are
eight interruptions. What can be decided together gets asked together.

**Propose instead of interrogate.** Anyone who has already named their evidence gets a finished
draft to correct, which is faster than describing an empty field.

**Never invent something to fill a field.** An empty certificate field is honest; an invented
number in the overview is the kind of damage that surfaces in the first client call. If evidence
is missing, say so and use the space differently.

**The video is optional, not required** (`references/video.md`). It pays off once the rest is in
place. A profile without a video goes live; a profile waiting on a video never does.
