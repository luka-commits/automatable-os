It reads your account before it asks you anything.

The add-on that turns Upwork from a place you check into a pipeline that runs. Below is what its setup does, and what happens every day once it is on.

What happens during its setup

It slots into the base setup after the equipment round. The principle: read first, then ask only what reading cannot answer.

1
setup-automatable-os

The connector is verified first

Before a single question it checks that the Upwork connector answers and fetches your account id. If the connector is missing, this is where it stops, because nothing downstream works without it and a half-connected setup is worse than an honest failure.

Connect it in the Claude app under Customize → Connectors, then restart the session. A connection made mid-session is invisible to that session, and that one step is behind most of the "it says it isn't connected" reports.

The button is greyed out, or the free slot is used up

Then add the server from the session you're already in, which doesn't touch the free plan's one-connector limit:

claude mcp add --transport http upwork https://mcp.upwork.com/mcp -s user

Then /mcp, pick upwork, choose Authenticate. On a Team or Enterprise account an owner has to release the connector once under Organization Settings, and until that happens no route works. Claude can run the command for you.

Stops here if missing
  • no silent half-setup
  • it tells you which step is missing
2

One question, three answers

Not a yes or no. A plain yes/no loses the third person, who answers no truthfully and then never hears about it again.

I work on Upwork

The account gets read. Everything below follows.

I want to start

Nothing to read yet, so the profile becomes the first thing you build.

I do not

The layer switches off for good. No tab, no skills, no nagging.

Leaves behind
  • upwork_enabled: true
  • or false, and it never asks again
3

Your account is read, not surveyed

Your profile, every past contract, the postings behind your wins, and the full text of every proposal you have already sent.

Those proposals are the most valuable input here and nobody has to type them: your writing, in the right register, on the right platform, already approved by you once. From twenty of them the system reads how you open, how you handle price, how you close.

Leaves behind
  • context/experience.md
  • context/testimonials.json
  • your track record, unrounded
4
upwork-profile

The profile gets audited, or built

This comes before the first search on purpose. The profile decides whether you appear in results at all and is the first thing a client opens after your cover letter.

Honest about its limits: the API can write availability, employment, languages and education, and nothing else. Title, overview, skills, rate, portfolio, video: all of that is handed to you as finished text to paste in.

Leaves behind
  • findings, each with what it costs you
  • finished text to paste
5

The niche interview, and only then

What reading could not answer: what a strong match looks like, which search tracks to run, and at least one real false-positive trap.

This is the file that stays empty if the interview is rushed, and an empty one produces a screener that scores everything alike.

Leaves behind
  • context/expertise.md
  • the file that makes the screener yours
The base setup comes first. This add-on has no day loop, no dashboard and no projects of its own; it fills the ones the base already has. That side is on its own page.

Then the chain runs, in this order

Each step feeds the next, and the last one hands over to the base.

1

Jobs get scored, not just listed

upwork-screener searches broadly and scores every result from 0 to 100 against your own niche file. Every real candidate is logged with a status, so the same job never surfaces twice. Strong matches can ping you on Slack.

2

A pitch page built for one job

upwork-pitch-page makes a single self-contained page: an interactive diagram of the solution the client can pan around, zoom into and rename nodes in, a slot for a walkthrough video you record yourself, your testimonials and your CV.

3

The proposal that actually gets submitted

upwork-proposal writes the cover letter and the screening answers: proof up front, quantified deliverables, risk reversal, a clear next step.

Record yourself solving the problem first and it uses that transcript as its source of real specifics, which reads far less generic than anything written from the posting alone.

4

Replies, while the thread is warm

upwork-inbox and upwork-reply handle client messages, invitations and offers. Drafts, never sends.

5

And then it stops being acquisition

upwork-won turns the job into a project in the base layer, with its own folder, its own first task and the material moved in.

Referrals and direct clients take that same route in, which is the point: the pipeline only counts as finished when the work is being delivered.

One extra tab, three views

It appears in the base layer's dashboard and disappears with the add-on.

ListEvery candidate the screener logged, sortable, with its score and status.
PipelineA board with one column each for outreach, in contact and offer sent. Cards move as the status changes.
StatsThe funnel, from found to applied to in contact to offer to won. This is the number that tells you whether the thing is working.
The dashboard never writes anything, here either. Buttons copy a ready sentence to your clipboard for you to paste into the chat. It stays a pure view.

What it will not and cannot do

The second half is not a design choice. It is Upwork's rules and Upwork's API.

Never submits a proposal by itselfchoice
Everything is a draft you read and send.
Never runs on a timertheir rules
Upwork treats background polling as scraping and unattended automation as grounds for closing an account. The screener runs when you ask for it and once inside your morning, and that's deliberate.
Cannot change your profiletheir API
The API refuses to write your title, overview, skills, rate, portfolio or intro video. The add-on reads all of it and hands you finished text; you paste it in on the site.
Cannot export a message threadtheir API
Reading gives a lot here, writing gives almost nothing. Where that limit bites, the docs say so in place.
Sends nothing outwardchoice
The one optional exception is a community dashboard you switch on yourself with a token, and it carries counters only: how many jobs found, applied, replied, won. No titles, no client names, no text of any kind. A dry run prints the exact bytes and sends nothing, so you can check before you believe it.

Where this sits

One add-on among others, on a base that works without any of them.

You never download two things and hope they fit. You set up the base, and it asks during setup whether you work through a channel an add-on covers. Say yes and it is switched on; say no and nothing about it appears anywhere.
