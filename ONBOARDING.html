It checks the connector answers, then reads before it asks: your profile, your past contracts, and the full text of proposals you have already sent. That last one is your writing, in the right register, already approved by you once.

Then three questions, and only three, because they are the ones no API can answer: what you want more of, what you would turn down today, and your name.

Your profile can carry an application

Before the first application, not after the tenth. The profile decides whether you appear in search results at all, and it is the first thing a client opens after your cover letter. Applying around a weak one makes every proposal more expensive than it needs to be.

Skipping steps 2 and 3

Say you do not work on Upwork and the whole acquisition layer stands down: no connector asked for, no reminders, no half-empty tab in the dashboard. The day loop, your projects and the delivery half are untouched, and a client from a referral takes exactly the same route into a project that a won job does.

Starting out is a third answer, not a soft no. Step 2 normally learns your niche by reading your account, and a new account has nothing to read. So it asks you instead, and step 3 stops being an audit and becomes the first piece of work: without a profile you do not appear in search results at all.

What you need, and what you do not

ThingNeeded forWithout it
Claude Code All of it Nothing runs. This is the one hard requirement
An Upwork connector Steps 2 and 3, the screener, proposals, the inbox The day loop, projects and the dashboard work exactly as they do with it
Node.js The dashboard renderer and the Tooling tab The briefing still runs in the chat; you lose the rendered page
Mail and calendar A briefing that knows your day, not just your tasks morning still runs and names in one line what it skipped
A private git remote The evening backup Everything works. Nothing is backed up, which is its own risk
gh and vercel Building the thing you are pitching and giving the client a live link The pitch page draws the plan instead of linking it, which is what it does for most jobs anyway
Google Workspace, Supabase Nothing gets installed. Setup only asks whether you use them The handbooks stay in the folder unread, and the commands get guessed at instead
Slack, Firecrawl, a scraping key Individual conveniences The skill that wanted it says so and carries on

The rule underneath all of that: a missing tool degrades a step, it never fails a run. Anything that would be installed gets asked about first, and no is a complete answer that gets recorded rather than asked again.
